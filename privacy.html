Privacy Policy

Pointr for macOS · Last updated 8 November 2026

Pointr does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Keystrokes

When you turn on the keystroke overlay, Pointr reads key presses using macOS Input Monitoring only to draw them on your screen for a few seconds. Keystrokes are never written to disk, logged, or sent anywhere. macOS does not pass password fields or other secure input to apps, so those are never seen.

Mouse and pointer

Pointer position and clicks are used only to draw the highlight, click ripples and spotlight on your screen. They are not recorded or transmitted.

Settings

Your preferences (colours, sizes, shortcuts) are stored locally on your Mac in the app's sandboxed preferences. They never leave your device.

No network, analytics or tracking

Pointr makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.
